CONCLUSION: PREVENT · AVOID · RECOVER

1. PREVENT

Break one of the 4 Coffman conditions upfront. The yellow-box rule enforces all-or-nothing allocation, breaking Hold and Wait.

2. AVOID

Dynamically evaluate movements before entry. Safe-state reservation guarantees complete departure space before admission.

3. RECOVER

Detect persistent cycles, bar entrance, deploy police preemption to rollback the lowest-cost victim vehicle, and restore normal flow.
